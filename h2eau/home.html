In order from $0 up

Start at the top. Check off what you've done.

Swap things as you can afford them. Use up what you have first. Here's a preview of each level; the full guide has the research behind every one.

Level 1 · $0

Free swaps

  • See what's in your water. DM me your zip for a free water report.
  • Stop heating food in plastic. Move it to glass or ceramic first.
  • Fresh or frozen over canned when you can, and reuse glass jars.
  • Change out of sweaty workout clothes right after.
  • Dust with a damp cloth, open windows, shoes off at the door.
  • Say no to paper receipts or wash your hands after.
Level 2 · Under $25

Small buys

  • Glass or stainless bottle instead of bottled water. See what bottled costs you →
  • Wood or bamboo cutting board.
  • Loose leaf tea and a metal steeper instead of "silky" bags.
  • 2 or 3 glass containers for leftovers to start.
  • Wood or stainless utensils for anything hot.
  • Cotton or wool socks.
Level 3 · $25 to $100

Kitchen & closet

  • Cast iron or stainless pan instead of nonstick.
  • Natural fiber workout gear for what you sweat in most.
  • Cotton or linen sheets, since you spend a third of your life in bed.
  • A filter certified for your water (get your report first).
Level 4 · $100+

The big upgrade

  • Upgrade your water at home. No more buying, carrying and recycling plastic bottles. One setup for the whole household.
  • A natural fiber wardrobe, over time. Replace pieces with cotton, linen, wool or hemp as they wear out.
